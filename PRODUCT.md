# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People managing personal finances across accounts, expenses, budgets, goals, income, recurring payments, and profile settings. They use the app as an everyday money cockpit where quick scanning and trustworthy balances matter more than decorative flourish.

## Product Purpose

SAM helps users understand current balances, spending, budgets, goals, and activity in one consistent finance app. Success means every number is traceable, every account mutation is financially coherent, and the interface stays readable across all supported themes.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, terminal-native. The product should feel focused and capable without feeling cold or generic.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style finance dashboards, oversized decorative cards, hard-to-read low-contrast terminal effects, and one-off controls that behave differently across screens.

## Design Principles

1. Financial state must be auditable: balances, transactions, budgets, and goals should match the user's mental model.
2. Visual systems should be token-driven so themes affect every screen consistently.
3. Dense information is allowed when it improves scanning, but hierarchy must stay obvious.
4. Interactions should use familiar controls and consistent save/cancel behavior.
5. Terminal aesthetics should support clarity, not obscure data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast for primary text and actionable UI. Preserve reduced-motion friendliness, avoid relying on color alone for state, and keep light and dark-like themes equally legible.
